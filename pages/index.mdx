This is the official documentation for the ESX Framework. The ESX Framework is an open source project that aims to bring the best FiveM framework on the market. Learn more on how to use it below.

Whatever your experience in developing fivem scripts is, we got you covered!

## Introduction

### Your first Server
We have a detailed text based tutorial on how to create a FiveM Server for ESX. Take a look at [The installation guide](tutorial/install) 

### Your first Script
Create your first script for FiveM and ESX following our [Developing guide](tutorial/developing)

## Troubleshoot
You are experiencing issues creating your ESX fivem server or a script? Take a look at the [Troubleshoot](troubleshoot) Page!.

Still having issues or something is unclear? Come join our [Discord](https://discord.esx-framework.org/') and get help from our Staff and other Users!
